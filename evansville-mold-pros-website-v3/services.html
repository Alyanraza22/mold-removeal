1. Tell Us What Is Happening

Call (930) 758 0114 or submit the website contact form. Tell us whether the mold is in a basement, crawl space, attic, bathroom, or another area.

2. Share Your Property Details

Provide your ZIP code and explain whether the area is still wet, whether there has been a leak, and when you first noticed the problem. If the form supports photo uploads, you can include pictures to help explain the concern.

3. Get Connected With an Independent Contractor

We use your request to help connect you with an independent mold remediation contractor serving your area. Availability depends on your address and the contractor's schedule.

4. Discuss the Problem and Get a Written Quote

The contractor can assess the reported issue and explain the proposed work. Ask what the quote includes, whether damaged materials may need removal, and whether repairs to the moisture source are included.

5. Review the Details Before Approving Work

Before making a decision, review the price, scope of work, schedule, and relevant qualifications. Ask for proof of insurance and references if you need them.

Evansville Mold Pros is a referral service. Independent contractors perform the assessment and remediation work, set their own prices, and confirm timing and job details directly with you.
