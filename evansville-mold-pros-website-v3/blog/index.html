When Should You Seek Professional Help?

Some moisture problems can be addressed with routine maintenance, while others may require further assessment. Consider seeking appropriate professional advice if mold keeps returning, building materials appear damaged, water has entered enclosed spaces, or you cannot identify the source of persistent dampness.

An inspection may help identify visible concerns and possible moisture sources. Mold testing may be useful when sampling can answer a specific question, but it is not necessary in every situation.
