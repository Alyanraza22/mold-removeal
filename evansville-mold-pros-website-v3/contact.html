How it works

What Happens After You Contact Us?

Evansville Mold Pros is a referral service. We connect property owners with independent mold remediation contractors rather than performing inspections, testing, or remediation directly.

After you submit a request:

01

Share the details of your property and concern.

02

We help you seek a connection with an independent contractor, subject to availability and service coverage.

03

The contractor discusses your situation and determines whether an assessment is appropriate.

04

If work is recommended, the contractor can provide pricing and a proposed scope for you to review.

The independent contractor confirms availability, appointment arrangements, pricing, qualifications, and the work offered. Submitting a request does not guarantee an appointment or a particular price.

For more information, visit our Our Process page.

Good to know

Before You Contact Us

If possible, be ready to explain:

Where you have noticed mold, dampness, or a musty odor
Whether the issue followed a leak, flooding, or heavy rain
Whether the problem has returned after previous cleaning or repairs
Your property's location and type

Do not disturb suspected mold just to gather more information. If you are dealing with significant water damage, sewage contamination, or an unsafe area, avoid entering the affected space and seek appropriate professional assistance.
